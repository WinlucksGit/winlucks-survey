About Winlucks 5

What is Winlucks?

Winlucks is a UAE marketplace and e-commerce platform designed to help local businesses connect with customers, showcase their products and services, promote offers, and encourage repeat business.

Why should my business join Winlucks?

Winlucks gives your business an additional channel to reach customers, increase visibility, promote your products and offers, generate sales, and encourage customers to return.

What types of businesses can join Winlucks?

Winlucks is designed for consumer-facing businesses across the UAE, including restaurants, cafes, salons, barbershops, laundries, grocery stores, retail shops, automotive services, electronics, fashion, beauty, and other eligible local businesses.

Is Winlucks only for large businesses?

No. Winlucks is designed to support businesses of different sizes, with a particular focus on helping local businesses and SMEs participate in digital commerce and customer engagement.

Is Winlucks available across the UAE?

Winlucks is being developed for the UAE market. Availability of specific services, campaigns, and features may vary by location and launch phase.

Customers & offers 9

Do I need an online store to join?

No. Winlucks can help eligible local businesses establish a digital presence and showcase their products, services, and offers to customers through the platform.

I already use Instagram, WhatsApp or other platforms. Why do I need Winlucks?

You do not need to replace your existing channels. Winlucks provides an additional way to reach customers, promote your business, generate transactions, and build repeat customer relationships.

How can Winlucks help me get more customers?

Winlucks is designed to promote participating businesses through marketplace discovery, offers, digital marketing, social media, referrals, promotional campaigns, and other customer-acquisition activities.

Can customers discover my business through Winlucks?

Yes. Winlucks is designed to help customers discover participating businesses, products, services, offers, and promotions available through the platform.

Can I promote special offers on Winlucks?

Yes. Participating merchants can use Winlucks to showcase eligible offers and promotions, subject to the platform's applicable terms and campaign requirements.

Do I have to offer discounts?

Not necessarily. However, attractive offers can help encourage new customers to discover and try your business.

Can I manage my products, services and offers?

Eligible merchants can manage applicable business information, products, services, pricing, and offers through the Winlucks merchant platform, subject to Winlucks' merchant terms and platform features.

Can I track my performance?

Where available, Winlucks merchant tools provide insights into relevant activity and performance to help merchants understand how customers are engaging with their business.

Does Winlucks guarantee sales?

No. Winlucks does not guarantee a specific number of customers or sales. The platform provides businesses with additional tools and opportunities to increase visibility, attract customers, and generate business.

Rewards 3

What are WL Coins?

WL Coins are part of the Winlucks rewards ecosystem, designed to reward eligible customer activity and encourage customers to continue engaging and shopping through Winlucks.

How do rewards benefit my business?

Rewards can give customers an additional reason to choose participating businesses and return for future purchases. The goal is not simply to reward a transaction, but to help build repeat customer relationships.

Does Winlucks offer giveaways or promotional campaigns?

Winlucks may offer eligible giveaways, contests, rewards, or promotional campaigns from time to time, subject to applicable terms, requirements, and approvals.

Costs & payments 3

How much does it cost to join Winlucks?

Merchant pricing, commissions, introductory offers, and optional services may vary depending on the applicable Winlucks merchant plan or campaign. All applicable charges will be communicated clearly before activation.

Are there any hidden charges?

Applicable fees and charges are disclosed according to the merchant's selected services and agreement. Merchants should review their applicable commercial terms before activation.

How and when do I receive payments?

Payment processing, settlement schedules, applicable fees, and payout arrangements are provided as part of the merchant onboarding and commercial terms.

Joining 5

What do I need to register as a merchant?

Registration requirements depend on the type of business and services offered. Winlucks may request valid business and licensing information, contact details, banking or settlement information, and other documents required for merchant verification and onboarding.

How long does merchant registration take?

Once the required information and documents are submitted, Winlucks will review the application and guide the merchant through the activation process.

Is my business automatically approved after registration?

No. Merchant applications may be subject to verification, eligibility requirements, documentation checks, and approval by Winlucks.

Why should I join Winlucks early?

Early participating merchants can establish their presence on the platform, prepare their products and offers, and be ready to benefit from Winlucks customer-acquisition and launch activities as they become available.

How do I become a Winlucks merchant?

Submit your merchant registration through Winlucks. Our team will guide you through verification, profile setup, product or service listing, and activation.
